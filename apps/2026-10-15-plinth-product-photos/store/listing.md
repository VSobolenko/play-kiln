### Full description (1544/4000)

Make product photos ready for the places you sell.

Plinth helps sellers turn their own product photos into polished listing images. Cut out a product, compose it on a clean or generated background, check marketplace image requirements, and export with a clear record of how the image was made.

• Keep the product's original pixels when composing a new background.
• See whether an image is original, manually adjusted, AI-enhanced, or uses a generated background.
• Review image dimensions and marketplace rules before exporting.
• Use the camera or choose photos from your device.

Start with 10 free credits per device. Processing that uses paid providers costs credits; one-time credit packs add more. A separate one-time Plinth Unlock removes the free export watermark and five-item batch limit; it does not include processing credits. There are no subscriptions. Review the credit cost and price shown in the app before confirming a purchase.

Photos you choose for server processing are sent to Plinth's server and its image-processing provider. Our server deletes uploaded photos within one hour and results within 24 hours; the provider may retain media for 30 days under its own policy. See the privacy policy for details.

You must have the rights to the photos you use. AI-generated content is labelled in the app and its export metadata; Plinth does not claim C2PA Content Credentials. You can report a generated result from inside the app.
