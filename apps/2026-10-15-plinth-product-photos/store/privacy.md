# Privacy policy — Plinth: Product Photos

**Last updated:** 2026-10-15

Plinth helps sellers prepare product photos for marketplace listings. This policy explains what
information is handled when you use the app and how to ask us to delete data associated with your
installation.

## Photos and image processing

When you choose to run a server-based cut-out, recognition, or image-generation operation, Plinth
sends the selected photo to the Plinth server and on to its image-processing provider, fal, to
complete that operation. The app reduces submitted images to no more than 2048 pixels on the longest
side. Photos are not sent for local-only work.

On Plinth's server, uploaded source files are deleted within one hour and processing results within
24 hours. The processing provider's own terms state that it may retain request inputs and outputs for
30 days. That provider-side retention is separate from Plinth's timers and outside Plinth's control.
Do not upload an image unless you have the rights to use it.

## Other information

The app sends listing text to Plinth's server and a text-generation provider only when you ask it to
generate listing copy; that text is not kept after the request. Do not include personal or sensitive
details in listing text. If you report a generated result,
your report note and a random installation/wallet identifier are sent to Plinth's server. Report
notes and the wallet data are kept until you request deletion.

When you make an in-app purchase, Google Play processes your payment details. Plinth receives
purchase information needed to grant credits or an unlock; a purchase token is sent to Plinth's
server for verification and is stored there as a hash. The app does not receive your card or other
payment instrument details.

Plinth uses a random installation identifier and wallet identifier to provide credits and associate
your app data. These are not hardware identifiers. No user account is required.

## Retention and deletion requests

To request deletion of data associated with your Plinth installation, open **Settings → Delete my
data** in the app and send the prepared email to **v.sobolenko.vs@gmail.com**. We may ask for
information needed to identify and verify the request. Deleting Plinth-held data does not erase
Google Play's transaction records or cause fal to delete provider-held media before its own
30-day retention period.

## Information Plinth does not collect

Plinth does not ask for personal details, read your location or contacts, use your microphone, or
require an account. Listing text is user-provided and could contain information you choose to enter;
do not include personal or sensitive details. The app
does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. GPS metadata is removed from
exported images.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** connect to Plinth's server for operations you start, listing-copy generation, and
  purchase-related functions.
- **Camera:** take a product photo when you choose to use the camera feature. You can instead select
  an existing photo.
- **Notifications:** deliver the batch-completion reminder if you enable notifications.
- **Network state:** apply the Wi-Fi-only upload preference.

## AI-generated content and image provenance

Generated backgrounds and AI-enhanced results are identified in the app and in exported provenance
metadata. Plinth does not claim to create C2PA Content Credentials. You can report an AI-generated
result from inside the app. You confirm that you have the rights to photos you use.

## Contact

For privacy questions or a deletion request, email **v.sobolenko.vs@gmail.com**.
