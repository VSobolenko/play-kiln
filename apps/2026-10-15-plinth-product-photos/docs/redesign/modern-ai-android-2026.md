# Modern AI Android apps and Compose building blocks — Plinth research

**Snapshot:** 2026-10-17. **Scope:** public product documentation/listings and the Plinth source tree; no competitor was installed or operated, and no app build was run.

## Evidence labels and limits of observation

- **MEASURED** — read directly from the repository or a source page during this task.
- **ON RECORD** — stated in a vendor's listing/help/docs or in the prior competitor teardown.
- **INFERRED** — a design interpretation of recorded evidence, not an observation of a running app.
- **UNVERIFIED** — this research could not observe the claim.

The phrase “first three seconds” is used as a design question, not as a timed usability test. Store pages and help articles establish which visual result or job an app foregrounds; they do **not** prove its actual launch screen, animation timing, first-run onboarding, device-specific layout, or navigation-bar height. None of the vendors' apps was captured to `.kiln/scratch/`; consequently no claim below is presented as a measured runtime/pixel observation.

## 2. Ready Compose libraries: allowlist recommendation

**MEASURED** — read `gradle/libs.versions.toml` and `app/build.gradle.kts`. The slot already pins AGP **9.4.1**, Kotlin/Compose compiler **2.3.21**, and Compose BOM **2026.09.00**. The factory's `AGENTS.md` maps that BOM to Compose UI **1.12.0** and Material 3 **1.4.0**. The slot already directly includes `androidx.compose.material3:material3`, Compose UI/graphics/tooling-preview, Material 3 adaptive Navigation 3, Navigation 3 **1.1.7**, Activity Compose, and the BOM. It does not declare a direct version for BOM-managed Compose libraries.

The app's existing production dependencies also include AndroidX Core KTX **1.19.0**, Lifecycle Compose **2.11.0**, Activity Compose **1.13.0**, AppCompat **1.7.1**, DataStore **1.2.1**, Room **2.8.5** (KSP **2.3.12**), WorkManager **2.12.0**, Paging runtime **3.5.3** and Paging Compose **3.5.1**, CameraX **1.6.2**, Kotlin serialization **1.11.0**, OkHttp **5.5.0**, Coil 3 Compose **3.6.3**, Play Billing **9.1.0** plus the local capability modules, Profile Installer **1.4.1**, Play Review **2.0.2**, and Play Integrity **1.6.0**. ## 3. Prioritised implementation backlog for later workers

Each row is a later implementation task, **not work done in this research pass**. Screen partitions are intentionally file-local where practical. New dependencies should be exceptional; use the current Compose/Coil classpath first. Do not overlap the sibling work on `ui/components/PlinthNavBar.kt`, `ui/wallet/WalletScreen.kt`, or `ui/settings/SettingsScreen.kt`.

1. **Make the next operation's price legible at the decision point.** Files: `ui/home/HomeScreen.kt`, `ui/components/PlinthToolCard.kt`, `ui/components/PlinthCostChip.kt`, and the relevant Home string resources. Dependency: **No** (reuse Compose and the existing price component). Effect: one visible, compact cost on the specific action card and a concise “what happens next” treatment, instead of requiring sellers to scan a prose price list; this addresses the owner's request for a polished, modern-feeling AI surface and reduces price surprise. **Boundary:** do not change the global navigation, Wallet, or Settings surfaces; coordinate the persistent balance with the sibling owner.
2. **Give creation and generation a deliberate motion/scroll rhythm.** Files: `ui/home/HomeSceneStrip.kt`, `ui/autocard/FormingCardPreview.kt`, `ui/autocard/AutoCardScreen.kt`. Dependency: **No** for the first pass; use Foundation pager/snap/animation and the current M3 theme. Effect: tactile scene-card snapping, gentle entry/selection transitions, and a result-shaped in-progress state that resolves into the image rather than a bare spinner or abrupt screen change. If a custom branded animation is later justified, evaluate Lottie separately and do not fake percentage progress.
3. **Turn Library into an image-first project browser.** Files: `ui/library/LibraryScreen.kt`, `ui/library/LibraryViewModel.kt`. Dependency: **No** (Coil is already present; Foundation list/grid APIs are on the BOM). Effect: a denser thumbnail-led library with clear project/output distinctions and usable empty/loading states, so “saved work” feels like a creative product rather than a plain list.
4. **Make Export feel like a finished handoff.** Files: `ui/export/ExportScreen.kt`, `ui/export/ExportPackSource.kt`. Dependency: **No**. Effect: present export destinations, format/size and provenance as a clear, visually prioritized finish step, with a strong completed state rather than a raw form.
5. **Make Coach feel like guided camera craft, not instructions in a document.** Files: `ui/coach/CoachScreen.kt`. Dependency: **No** (CameraX is already installed). Effect: make the next shot, framing guidance and capture feedback visually scannable while the seller holds the phone, addressing the “raw/scant” impression in a hands-on screen.
6. **Polish Batch as a live queue.** Files: `ui/batch/BatchScreens.kt`, `ui/batch/BatchViewModel.kt`. Dependency: **No** (WorkManager and Compose are present). Effect: make queued/running/finished/failed jobs distinct at a glance, with visible retry/resume affordances and image thumbnails instead of a text-heavy batch status list.
7. **Make Slide template browsing image-led and browsable.** Files: `ui/slide/SlideKitScreen.kt`. Dependency: **No**. Effect: show the actual template/layout choices as dense preview cards and use controlled selection/preview motion, so “smart scrolling” has content to browse rather than rows of labels.
8. **Make Cutout’s before/after and charge/result state unmistakable.** Files: `ui/cutout/CutoutScreen.kt`, `ui/cutout/CutoutViewModel.kt`. Dependency: **No** (Coil and Compose are present). Effect: visually compare the user's input and returned cutout, keep progress anchored to the image bounds, and make the charged operation/result transition understandable without duplicating Wallet work.
9. **Give Editor controls a clear hierarchy around the product image.** Files: `ui/editor/EditorScreen.kt`, `ui/editor/EditorViewModel.kt`. Dependency: **No**. Effect: make the composed product the hero, group editing actions into readable tool families, and use a clear selection/undo state instead of a flat control list.
10. **Give Listing a composed preview alongside text.** Files: `ui/listing/ListingTextScreen.kt`. Dependency: **No**. Effect: make marketplace copy feel connected to the listing card/product image, and expose copy/validation states as compact editorial feedback rather than a bare text utility.
11. **Make Group feel like a batchable SKU workspace.** Files: `ui/group/GroupScreen.kt`, `ui/group/GroupViewModel.kt`. Dependency: **No**. Effect: make groups, SKU names and included product imagery scannable so grouping reads as a real seller workflow, not a generic data-entry screen.
12. **Polish Card around an image-led, inspectable result.** Files: `ui/card/CardScreen.kt`, `ui/card/CardViewModel.kt`. Dependency: **No**. Effect: prioritize the rendered card, make the marketplace target and next export action obvious, and use consistent result/selection feedback to replace the current “MVP utility” feel.
13. **Add controlled display typography and surface depth without changing the palette.** Files: `ui/theme/Type.kt`, `app/src/main/res/font/` plus the corresponding font licence asset, and only the selected screen's typography references after review. Dependency: **No Maven dependency** if bundling a licence-cleared Noto Serif Display file. Effect: give hero/result headlines a distinct typographic voice and improve hierarchy; retain Plinth's existing palette, sans-serif body text, dynamic sizing/wrapping and numeric legibility. Do not replace the brand with default Material colors or add an unapproved icon pack.

## 4. Honest limits

- This is desk research from vendor listings/help pages and existing repository research, not a competitor-app usability study. No live Android sessions, navigation taps, screen recordings, or screenshot measurements were made. **Motion, actual first-three-second sequence, rendered image density, bar height, settings labels and in-context price position remain unverified unless explicitly documented above.**
- A Play listing is marketing copy. It can evidence what a vendor claims or promotes, not that its current app build renders that claim as a particular screen or that a given pattern causes the user to feel “modern.”
- A library's existence, stable version and permissive licence do not prove it will improve Plinth. Review its transitive dependencies, binary cost, accessibility/reduced-motion behavior, asset rights and compatibility in the real slot before adoption. No Gradle build was run as part of this research.
- These sources cannot prove taste, usability, retention, conversion, revenue, or whether the owner will like a proposed visual treatment. That requires a real implementation, on-device review and the owner's product decision. No install-count claim or competitor performance comparison is made here.
