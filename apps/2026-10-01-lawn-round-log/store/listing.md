### Full description (2471/4000)

Lawn Round Log is the round book a solo mower actually owns.

It is not an account and not a cloud dashboard. Your customers, the order you drive them in, every visit you log and every payment you take live on your phone, in one record you keep. Open it at the kerb in airplane mode and it works: no login, no sync, no server that has to be up before you can start.

THE ROUND, IN YOUR ORDER
Add each customer once and move the list into the order you actually drive. Nothing asks you for an appointment, a time slot or a calendar entry. The round is the worklist, and it stays in the order you set it.

ONE TAP AT THE KERB
Mark a visit mowed, skipped or rained off with a single tap. Every control is at least 48 dp, and each status is written out as a word as well as coloured, so you can read it one-handed in the sun.

RAINED OFF? PUSH THE DAY
One action moves every customer due today forward exactly one day. Each one moves once, so nobody is moved twice and nobody is left behind, and a push on a later day moves them one further day.

WHO OWES YOU
Each customer carries a running balance built from his billable visits minus the payments you record. Mowed visits count; skipped and rained-off visits never do. Record a payment in one tap and remove it if you mistyped. The total owed across the whole round is on screen, and every figure is recomputed from the stored visits and payments, so it is identical after a restart.

KEEP YOUR OWN COPY
Export the stored record to a CSV file through the system file picker, and the app tells you how many rows it wrote. Edit or delete a customer, a visit or a payment; deleting more than one record asks first and names what will be removed; that confirmation is on by default and can be turned off in Settings.

WHAT YOU PAY
The app is free for up to eight customers, with no time limit and no trial clock. One purchase adds two things and nothing else: unlimited customers and the CSV export. The visit history and the owed balances are not part of the purchase — they are in the free app, and the unlock never takes them away. One price, paid once. There is no subscription, no renewal and no advertising anywhere in the app, and the purchase can be restored after a reinstall.

PRIVATE BY CONSTRUCTION
The record stays on your phone. No account is created, no analytics run, and nothing you type is uploaded. The only party involved beyond you and your customers is Google Play, and only to take the one-time payment.
