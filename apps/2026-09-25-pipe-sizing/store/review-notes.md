# Reviewer notes — Gas Pipe Sizing Workbook

No account, login, invitation, or demo credentials are required. The release is paid once through the store listing; the app contains no ads, subscription, in-app purchase, or billing capability.

## Review journey

1. Launch the app.
2. Enter a load, developed length, fitting allowance, unit selection, and user-confirmed capacity-like value.
3. Record the edition, table, and row citation.
4. Review the arithmetic and calculation-aid disclaimer.
5. Remove one required input to see guidance identify what must be corrected.

The app performs arithmetic and bookkeeping only. It supplies no code-table data and does not determine compliance or certify work. It restores the current case after the process ends by reading it back from local storage, and keeps no history of previous cases.

## Permissions, data, and media

The manifest declares no permissions. No data is collected, transmitted, or shared; current input is local to the device. Generated store screenshots are synthetic renderer outputs, not device captures, and must be replaced with truthful captures before publishing if the final visual differs.

Version reviewed: `0.1.0` (code `1`)
