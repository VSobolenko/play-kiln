## Limitations — read before acting on this

- **Ratings only.** The scan reads the rating a card shows. It does not read install counts, review counts, or review sentiment, so "demand proven" means "enough installs exist to compute a rating", not "this makes money".
- **The result set is display-capped (~30 cards).** That is a page limit, not a competitor count, and not a market size. A query with 30 visible results does not have 30 competitors.
- **One storefront, one session.** Signed-out `hl=en`, `gl=US` only. Play search is personalised and geo-dependent, so this is a spot check of a single storefront, not a market study.
- **Verdicts are definitions applied to ratings, not revenue forecasts.** `candidate` means demand is proven and the visible supply is inadequate by the stated thresholds; it does not predict installs, revenue, or conversion.
- **Reputation is invisible.** The screen cannot tell that an incumbent is a Red Cross or a government body, which is why organisation-like publisher names are surfaced as a hint for a human instead of being scored.
- **A rating is a lagging signal.** A weak incumbent may be weak because the market will not pay, and a fortress may have an unserved sub-niche that this query text does not reach.
- **Thin result sets.** These queries returned fewer than 15 visible cards, so their shares rest on a small sample and their verdict is weaker evidence than one computed over ~30: `personal trainer client tracker` (13), `nurse shift handoff` (12), `truck driver expense log` (12), `gig driver earnings tracker` (13), `warhammer army list` (13), `dnd initiative tracker` (10), `pickleball scoreboard` (13), `horse riding log` (14), `self employed mileage log` (10).
- **Token-polluted queries.** Play search is a fuzzy text match, so these queries returned apps that do not answer them — and a verdict is arithmetic over the apps it was given, so these labels describe the wrong shelf: `personal trainer client tracker` (polluted, 4/13 titles echo the query), `teacher gradebook` (partial, 16/30 titles echo the query), `nurse shift handoff` (partial, 6/12 titles echo the query), `warhammer army list` (partial, 9/13 titles echo the query), `dnd initiative tracker` (partial, 6/10 titles echo the query), `scale model paint log` (partial, 17/30 titles echo the query), `self employed mileage log` (partial, 10/10 titles echo the query).
- **Ceiling on inference.** 31 queries screened; 21 reported a pocket. The manual baseline in `docs/niche-selection.md` is roughly one candidate in four, so a hit rate far above that is a reason to widen the query set, not to trust the label.
