# MeshMend — prepare an STL for printing on the phone

## Decision in one page

**INFERRED — category and job:** This is a 3D-print preparation app for hobbyists who already have an STL or 3MF model: import it, inspect and repair mesh defects, estimate print risks, fit it to a bed, and export a changed file that can be opened by a slicer.

**INFERRED — paying user and offer:** A hobbyist who would rather fix one downloaded model on the phone than open desktop repair software pays **$9.99 once** to unlock model repair, print-risk analysis, bed-fit transforms, and export; payment appears after the free import and diagnostic preview, immediately before the first repair or prepared-file export.

**INFERRED — wedge, in one sentence:** Unlike the measured Android shelf of mostly STL viewers, CAD viewers, printer companions, and model creators, MeshMend turns the user's existing mesh into a repaired, print-risk-annotated, bed-fitted file locally and hands that changed file to the user's slicer.

**ON RECORD — form constraint:** The owner's 2026-10-08 recon brief forbids a record book, log, ledger, journal, checklist, register, or CRUD table and requires a transformed file plus a print/cut verdict.

## Money and monetization decision

**INFERRED — archetype:** `purchase_led_single_unlock` fits a durable local tool whose core value is a single repair-and-export capability and whose file processing has no per-use server cost; one payment unlocks all preparation tools and has no expiry or renewal.

| Candidate model | Decision and revenue reason |
|---|---|
| `purchase_led_single_unlock` | **INFERRED — selected:** a single unlock monetizes the complete transformation pipeline without recurring delivery cost; $9.99 is a test price, not a measured Play price. |
| `purchase_led_repeat_sales` | **INFERRED — rejected:** a repaired mesh is a durable output and local algorithms cost no marginal fulfilment, so there is no evidenced recurring consumable to sell without inventing artificial packs. |
| `ads_led_utility` | **INFERRED — rejected:** model repair and file export are occasional, focused tasks, so repeat ad impressions are unlikely to offset the cost of this large build; ads would also interrupt file handling. |
| `purchase_only_no_ads` | **INFERRED — rejected:** all eight enriched cards in the tested Play set were free downloads, so charging before a user can inspect their own model would discard the observed free-install funnel without measured paid-download precedent. |
| Subscription | **ON RECORD — excluded:** subscriptions are prohibited in the owner's 2026-10-08 brief and are not an available economics archetype. |

**INFERRED — unit arithmetic (USD):** At a proposed $9.99 price, applying an assumed 15% Play service fee and an 8% tax haircut to the sticker price gives `$9.99 × (1 − 0.15) × (1 − 0.08) = $7.81` estimated net per sale. The 8% tax haircut is a conservative modeling assumption, not a measured tax rate or tax treatment; actual proceeds depend on Play's fee tier, tax location, refunds, and currency.

**INFERRED — target arithmetic:** To clear a stated $500/month net-revenue target before development cost, support, refunds beyond the modeled haircut, and acquisition spend requires `ceil($500 ÷ $7.81) = 65` one-time unlock sales per month, yielding about `$507.65` at the rounded net estimate.

**UNVERIFIED — commercial hurdle:** The 65-sale monthly target is not forecast demand; no measured conversion rate, IAP product price, or one-off purchase rate was available from this Play scan. If pricing research cannot establish a plausible route to 65 monthly buyers, the revenue case fails even though the category demand signal is real.

**MEASURED — numeric kill criteria proposed for the economics plan:** At day 90, require at least 500 installs and at least $100 gross in recorded proceeds; `docs/ideation/plans/mesh-mend.economics.json` carries these finite thresholds.

## App behavior and build honesty

**INFERRED — import and inspection:** Use Android Storage Access Framework `ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT` for STL, OBJ, and 3MF input, parse the mesh locally, and show a touchable `GLSurfaceView`/OpenGL ES preview with part selection, bounds, units, normals, and an error overlay; there is no account or upload.

**INFERRED — geometry pipeline:** Build a half-edge/adjacency representation; detect boundary loops, duplicate faces, non-manifold edges, disconnected shells, degenerate triangles, and inconsistent normals; show a repair preview and preserve the original until the user accepts the changed mesh.

**INFERRED — printability checks:** Estimate minimum wall thickness by ray casting, flag overhangs by face-normal angle, show support-risk regions, compute volume and bounding dimensions, and warn when the chosen bed bounds are exceeded. These are geometry heuristics rather than a slicer simulation or a guarantee of a successful print.

**INFERRED — transforms and output:** Provide uniform scaling, axis orientation and lay-flat operations, simple hollowing with drain-hole placement, and a multi-part plate layout; export a changed STL/3MF with `ACTION_CREATE_DOCUMENT`, and offer `ACTION_SEND` through `FileProvider` so a slicer can receive the prepared file.

**UNVERIFIED — format and correctness boundary:** STL carries no units, arbitrary meshes can be extremely large, and automatic hole closure can change intended geometry; the builder must document unit assumptions, bound memory use, test malformed and adversarial fixtures, and never label a heuristic result as a print guarantee.

**INFERRED — detailed agent-hour estimate:** File SAF and format parsing, 24–36 hours; the OpenGL preview and touch interaction, 18–28; mesh topology and diagnostics, 14–22; hole/normal repair and review preview, 18–30; ray-cast thickness and overhang heuristics, 18–28; scale/orient/hollow/plate operations, 20–32; verified file export and sharing, 8–14; accessible multilingual UI, billing, settings, tests, performance work, and device verification, 24–36. The estimated total is **144–226 agent hours**, not a measured build time. It is owner information only: the job is neither reduced nor refused for its size, and its decision rests on the revenue arithmetic.

**ON RECORD — no server:** The owner's 2026-10-08 brief prefers a fully on-device file-and-maths job and prohibits client-side paid APIs; this proposal needs no server, account, network request, or paid API.

## Permissions and device capabilities

### Permissions

**INFERRED — manifest:** No runtime permission is needed for SAF document selection or user-initiated share/export. Do not request broad storage access, camera, network, or notification permissions for this job.

### Device capabilities

- camera: not used
- photo picker / gallery: not used — mesh inputs are documents, not photos.
- media playback or capture: not used
- microphone: not used
- location: not used
- motion sensors: not used
- contacts / calendar: not used
- notifications / reminder: not used
- home-screen widget: not used
- share / export: used — `Intent.ACTION_SEND` with a `FileProvider` content URI hands the changed mesh to the user's slicer; without it, the prepared file cannot leave the app through a share target; manifest permission: none.
- Bluetooth / USB: not used
- files / SAF: used — `ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT` imports and `ACTION_CREATE_DOCUMENT` exports the user's model; without it, the maker cannot bring an existing model in or save the transformed file; manifest permission: none (no storage runtime permission).

**INFERRED — delivery contract:** `docs/ideation/plans/mesh-mend.differentiators.json` carries five deferred promises across `before`, `during`, and `after`, with two core transformations. Its `data_out` promise remains `deferred` until a real proof artifact exists; the intended shipped proof must contain `ACTION_SEND` and `FileProvider`, rather than claiming a handoff already implemented.

**INFERRED — visual direction:** Use a dark graphite inspection bench with a lime/amber mesh-risk overlay, a unique wireframe “M” mark, and a repair timeline that scrubs original-to-repaired geometry instead of presenting a stock file-list screen.

**MEASURED — naming search:** `node scripts/scan-titles.mjs --query "MeshMend STL Repair" --query "MeshMend" ...` surfaced no exact title `MeshMend: STL Repair`; the standalone `MeshMend` query returned 12 mostly unrelated results and a polluted relevance guard, so the brand is not treated as trademark-cleared or search-proven.

**UNVERIFIED — naming and legal risk:** The naming plan records a five-candidate field, a chosen title score over 70, a rejected category title, and Play search observations; USPTO, EUIPO, WIPO, and Russian trademark registers were not searched, and the coined carrier still requires legal and locale review.

## Unknowns and falsifier

- **UNVERIFIED — willingness to pay:** whether users will pay $9.99 once for local repair when the measured Android offers are free with ads/IAP; decide by reading current in-app purchase sheets and testing paid-offer intent with actual 3D-print users.
- **UNVERIFIED — mobile performance:** whether representative phones can render and repair large meshes within usable memory and latency; decide with adversarial mesh fixtures and real-device profiling before release.
- **UNVERIFIED — repair safety:** whether automatic hole closure, wall-thickness ray casting, and hollowing preserve intended geometry across real user files; decide by comparing outputs against desktop repair tools and print test parts.
- **UNVERIFIED — desktop competition and price:** MeshLab's public site confirms active desktop mesh tooling and 3MF support, but this recon did not establish current licenses/prices for the desktop field or quantify how many Android makers already use desktop slicers.
- **UNVERIFIED — revenue:** whether the category can reach 65 monthly unlocks at the proposed price; decide with actual paid conversion and proceeds, not IAP badges or installs alone.

**Falsifier:** This proposal flips to **FAIL** if a repeat live Play title scan for `stl file repair` shows fewer than 18 of 30 visible results are genuinely about STL/3D-print model files after hand classification, or if a Play purchase-sheet check finds no one-time paid feature comparable to this job and user testing cannot support 65 $9.99 unlocks per month. It also fails if the apparent print-prep job reduces to viewing/conversion without a defensible, testable changed-file verdict.
