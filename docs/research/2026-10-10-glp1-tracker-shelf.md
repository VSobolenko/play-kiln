## Why this shelf, and why now

Two October 2026 chart readings said the factory should look at what people are
installing *this season*, not at another evergreen occupational log:

- Daily Google Play movement reports for 2026-10-08 and 2026-10-09 show AI
  image/video makers and AI home-design apps climbing several countries at once
  (Hailuo, Muselab, Kling, Room Design AI, RoomGPT). Those shelves pay, and they
  are refused below as thin paid-API wrappers.
- Health and fitness is the 2025–2026 consumer category that is still growing:
  ConsultMyApp's US iPhone comparison puts Health/Fitness/Wellness downloads
  **+27.3%** between late 2025 and summer 2026, and Sensor Tower records Health
  and Fitness in-app purchases at **$4.5 billion** in 2025. That is category
  weather, not a GLP-1 P&L.

The GLP-1 drug class (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound and compounded
semaglutide/tirzepatide) went mass-primary-care from 2024. In **April 2026**
MyFitnessPal added a free GLP-1 Support tier; on **2026-05-05** MyNetDiary
launched a paid GLP-1 Companion at **$9.99/month**. A category the fortress
apps are still bolting modules onto in spring 2026 is a *current* category, not
a 2015 habit-tracker leftover.

This memo is the evidence file `economics.json` and `differentiators.json` point
at.

## What the complaints add up to

INFERRED from the three cards:

1. The job is real and weekly. People log a shot, a site, a side effect, a
   weight, a plate. They will pay *something* — Shotsy is on a US grossing chart
   and MeAgain/Pep sell subscriptions.
2. The tax they refuse is **another monthly bill** on top of a drug that already
   consumes "all of my extra income".
3. The failure they cannot forgive is **losing the shot history**.
4. The AI calorie/meal guess is a complaint, not a request. Do not build it.
5. The clinic visit is implied ("notes to discuss with your healthcare
   provider" is MeAgain's own copy) and not delivered as a file the user can
   hand over without an account.

## Recipe this memo supports

**DoseFold Clinic Log** — an offline, no-account GLP-1 *clinic packet*: one-tap
shot + site map, side effects timed to hours-since-shot, a plate *photograph*
the user captioned (no AI calories), crash-safe local history, and a dated
PDF/CSV for the next endocrinology visit. One-time unlock. Not a medical
device. No pharmacokinetic "levels" chart (that is how Pep and Shotsy
differentiate, and it is a claims surface we do not need).

Wedge, one sentence: a person on a weekly GLP-1 shot keeps every dose, site,
side-effect and plate photo on the phone without an account, and leaves with a
dated clinic PDF — without a subscription and without an AI that guesses the
meal.
